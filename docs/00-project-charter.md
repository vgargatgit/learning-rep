# Project charter

## Purpose

Build a memorable but rigorous guided reading of **Learning representations by back-propagating errors**. The learning experience should resemble an illustrated technical book rather than a conventional paper summary.

## Audience

Experienced software engineers and beginning-to-intermediate ML practitioners who can follow algebra and basic derivatives but may not yet have an intuitive model of gradient flow through a multilayer network.

## Teaching contract

1. **Cartoons provide roles and relationships.** They do not redefine mathematical terms.
2. **Toy calculations stay small enough to recompute by hand.**
3. **Every equation has a dependency story.** Readers should know what depends on what before differentiating.
4. **Executable calculations are canonical.** Visuals must match code-generated values.
5. **Historical claims are separated from modern interpretation.**
6. **The project ends with implementation and verification, not only intuition.**

## Definition of done

A lesson is complete only when it contains:

- a learning objective;
- one conceptual diagram;
- one toy calculation or trace;
- precise definitions of new terms;
- at least one misconception check;
- executable verification where arithmetic is involved;
- a short bridge to modern neural-network practice.
